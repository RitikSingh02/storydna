Heiress Rhea hides her family name and starts working as a night receptionist at her father's luxury hotel to find out who is leaking guest data. The duty manager, Colin, humiliates her in front of the staff and calls her a useless temp. Rhea quietly installs a hidden recorder in the manager's office and finds that Colin sells guest lists to a rival hotel chain. Colin learns she is investigating him and frames her for the theft of a guest's jewellery. At the hotel's anniversary gala Rhea plays the recording on the ballroom screen and announces that she is the owner's daughter. Colin is escorted out by police and the staff apologise.
