Saas aur chachiyaan har family dinner par Dev ka mazaak udaati hain aur Riya se kehti hain ki talaak le le. Dev chupchaap family ka pichhla kiraya bhar deta hai. Jab makan malik ghar khali karwane ki dhamki deta hai, tab pata chalta hai ki asli malik Dev hi hai. Dinner par Dev eviction notice phaad kar sabke saamne bata deta hai ki building uski hai. Riya ko ehsaas hota hai ki ye shaadi ab sach ho chuki hai aur saas ko maafi maangni padti hai.
